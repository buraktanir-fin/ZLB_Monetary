inﬂation  would  be  more  ﬁrmly  anchored  than  has  been  typical  over  the  past  twenty  years,  so  uncertainty

uncertainty about inﬂation is higher than typical now and it may take some time to make the transition to a 
more appropriate anchoring of expectations.  Thus on balance, uncertainty about inﬂation would be broadly 
balanced.
